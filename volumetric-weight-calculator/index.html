Volumetric weight formula

Volumetric weight (kg) = L × W × H (cm) ÷ divisor Chargeable weight = higher of (actual weight, volumetric weight) Then rounded up to the courier's slab, usually 0.5 kg

Example: a 40 × 30 × 20 cm box weighing 3 kg has a volumetric weight of 24,000 ÷ 5,000 = 4.8 kg. You pay for 5 kg, not 3.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my courier charging more than the actual weight?

Light but bulky parcels take up space in the vehicle or aircraft, so carriers charge on volume. If your box is too big for its contents, you pay for air.

Does Amazon or Flipkart use volumetric weight?

Yes. Marketplace shipping fees use the higher of actual and volumetric weight (usually ÷5000), so packaging size directly affects your fees.

How do I measure a parcel?

Measure the longest side as length, then width and height, at the widest points including any bulges. Round each up to the next whole centimetre.
