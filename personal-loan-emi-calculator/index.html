The real cost of a personal loan

Lenders advertise the interest rate, but you also pay a processing fee (1–3% of the loan) plus 18% GST on that fee, deducted from the amount you receive. This calculator adds both, so you see the full cost.

Total cost = Total interest + Processing fee + 18% GST on fee

Example: ₹5 lakh at 12% for 3 years has an EMI of ₹16,607 and total interest of ₹97,858. A 2% fee adds ₹11,800 including GST, so the true cost is ₹1,09,658.

Frequently asked questions

What credit score do I need?

Most lenders want a CIBIL score of 700+. 750+ gets you the lowest rates.

Can I prepay a personal loan?

Usually after 6–12 EMIs, with a foreclosure charge of 2–5% plus GST. Check your loan agreement.

Flat rate vs reducing rate?

Some lenders quote a flat rate, which looks lower but is charged on the full original amount. A 10% flat rate is roughly an 18% reducing rate. This calculator uses the reducing-balance method.

These figures are estimates for planning only and are not financial advice. Actual returns, rates and charges depend on your bank, fund or scheme. Check the latest rates before you invest or borrow.
