How to write a quotation that wins the job

  1. Itemise everything. Break the work into clear lines (material, labour, delivery, installation) so the customer can see where the money goes.
  2. Show GST separately. Business customers want to see the tax they can claim back. Use the right HSN or SAC code.
  3. Set a validity date. Material prices change. 15–30 days is common.
  4. Spell out payment terms. For example, 50% advance and the balance on delivery, with your bank or UPI details.
  5. Follow up within 48 hours. Most quotes are lost to silence, not price.

Frequently asked questions

Is this quotation maker free?

Yes. Your first quotation PDF is free. Nothing you type is stored; the quotation disappears once you download it.

Can I convert the quotation into an invoice?

Once the customer accepts, open our free GST invoice generator to raise the tax invoice with the same items.

Should a quotation have GST?

If you are GST-registered, yes. Show the rate and amount separately. If you are not registered, untick "Charge GST".

How do I send the quotation on WhatsApp?

Download the PDF, then attach it in WhatsApp. On a phone, choose Print → Save as PDF, then Share → WhatsApp.
