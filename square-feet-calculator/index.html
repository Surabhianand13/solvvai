How to calculate square feet

Area (sq ft) = Length (ft) × Width (ft) Feet + inches: 12 ft 6 in = 12 + 6 ÷ 12 = 12.5 ft From metres: sq m × 10.764 = sq ft

Example: a room 12 ft 6 in × 10 ft is 12.5 × 10 = 125 sq ft. For L-shaped rooms, split them into rectangles, add each as a separate row, and the total is added up for you.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between carpet, built-up and super built-up area?

Carpet area is the usable floor inside the walls. Built-up area adds the wall thickness and balconies (usually 10–15% more). Super built-up adds a share of common areas like lobbies and lifts (often 25–35% more than carpet). Under RERA, flats must be sold on carpet area.

How many tiles do I need for a room?

Divide the floor area by the area of one tile, then add 5–10% for cutting and breakage. A 2 ft × 2 ft tile covers 4 sq ft, so a 125 sq ft room needs about 32 + 10% ≈ 35 tiles.

How do I measure an irregular plot?

Split it into rectangles and triangles. Add the rectangles here; a triangle's area is half of base × height. For legal use, rely on a licensed surveyor.
