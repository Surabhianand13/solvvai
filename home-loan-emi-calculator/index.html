How your home loan EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Example: ₹50 lakh for 20 years at 8.5% gives an EMI of ₹43,391. You repay ₹1.04 crore in total, so interest is ₹54.14 lakh.

Ways to reduce your home loan cost

  • Prepay early: part-payments in the first 5–7 years cut interest the most.
  • Increase EMI with salary hikes: even 5% a year shortens a 20-year loan by several years.
  • Balance transfer: moving to a lender 0.5% cheaper can save lakhs on a large loan.
  • Keep a good CIBIL score: 750+ gets you the best rates.

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim tax benefits on a home loan?

Under the old tax regime: up to ₹1.5 lakh on principal under Section 80C and up to ₹2 lakh on interest for a self-occupied home under Section 24(b). The new regime does not allow these for self-occupied homes.

Is there a penalty for prepaying a home loan?

No. RBI does not allow prepayment penalties on floating-rate home loans taken by individuals.

Fixed or floating rate?

Most Indian home loans are floating and linked to the repo rate. Fixed rates are usually higher and often reset after a few years.
