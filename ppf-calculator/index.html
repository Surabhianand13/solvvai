How PPF interest is calculated

Balance each year = (Previous balance + Deposit) × (1 + rate)

Interest is calculated monthly on the lowest balance between the 5th and the end of each month, and credited once a year on 31 March. Depositing before 5 April earns the most.

Example: ₹1.5 lakh a year for 15 years at 7.1% grows to about ₹40.68 lakh: ₹22.5 lakh invested and ₹18.18 lakh tax-free interest.

Withdrawals and loans from PPF

  • Partial withdrawal: once a year from the 7th financial year, up to 50% of the balance at the end of the 4th preceding year or the preceding year, whichever is lower.
  • Loan against PPF: from the 3rd to the 6th financial year, up to 25% of the balance at the end of the second preceding year, at 1% above the PPF rate.
  • Premature closure: allowed after 5 years for serious illness, higher education or a change in residency status, with 1% lower interest.

How to open a PPF account

Open it at any post office or at most banks, including SBI, HDFC, ICICI and Axis, often online through net banking. You need PAN, Aadhaar, a photo and a minimum ₹500 deposit. Each person can have only one PPF account; you can open another for a minor child.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extend PPF after 15 years?

Yes, in blocks of 5 years, with or without fresh deposits. Submit Form H within a year of maturity to continue contributing.

Can NRIs open a PPF account?

No new accounts, but an account opened while resident can continue until maturity.

PPF or ELSS?

PPF is guaranteed and tax-free but locked for 15 years. ELSS mutual funds have a 3-year lock-in and higher potential returns, with market risk.

Can I deposit more than ₹1.5 lakh in PPF?

No. Deposits above ₹1.5 lakh in a financial year (across your own and your minor child’s account) earn no interest and are not tax-deductible.

What happens if I miss a yearly PPF deposit?

The account becomes discontinued. You can revive it by paying ₹500 for each missed year plus a ₹50 penalty per year.

Is PPF better than an FD?

For long-term savers in higher tax brackets, yes: PPF interest is tax-free while FD interest is taxed at your slab rate. FDs are more flexible.
