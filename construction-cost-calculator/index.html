Frequently asked questions

Is construction cost lower per sq ft for more floors?

Usually, yes. The foundation and roof are shared, so a G+1 house often costs 5–10% less per sq ft than a single-storey house of the same total area.

What is the labour cost share?

Labour is typically 25–30% of total construction cost. With a turnkey contractor, labour and material are both included in the per-sq-ft rate.

Does this include interiors?

No. Modular kitchen, wardrobes, furniture and decor are usually ₹800–2,000 per sq ft extra depending on the finish.
