How GST is calculated

Adding GST to a price before tax:

GST amount = Price × GST rate ÷ 100 Total price = Price + GST amount

Removing GST from a price that already includes it:

Price before GST = Inclusive price ÷ (1 + GST rate ÷ 100) GST amount = Inclusive price − Price before GST

Example: an item sold for ₹1,180 including 18% GST has a base price of 1180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 and GST of ₹180. In the same state that is ₹90 CGST + ₹90 SGST. Across states it is ₹180 IGST.

Input tax credit (ITC) explained

GST is charged at every stage, but you only pay tax on the value you add. A trader buys goods for ₹10,000 + ₹1,800 GST and sells them for ₹15,000 + ₹2,700 GST. The trader pays the government only ₹900 (₹2,700 collected − ₹1,800 already paid). To claim ITC, the supplier must have filed their return and the invoice must appear in your GSTR-2B.

GST formulas for Excel or Google Sheets

Add GST: =A2*(1+B2/100) (A2 = price, B2 = rate) GST amount: =A2*B2/100 Remove GST: =A2/(1+B2/100) GST included: =A2-A2/(1+B2/100)

Reverse charge (RCM)

For some services, such as goods transport by a GTA, legal services from an advocate, or services from an unregistered supplier in notified cases, the buyer pays GST directly to the government instead of the supplier. The invoice should say "tax payable on reverse charge".

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the inclusive price by (1 + rate ÷ 100). For ₹1,180 at 18%: 1180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000 before GST, so the GST is ₹180. Choose "Remove GST" above and the calculator does it for you.

Is GST charged on the discounted price?

Yes. If the discount is shown on the invoice at the time of sale, GST is charged on the price after the discount.

Do I need to register for GST?

You generally need to register once your yearly turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services (lower limits apply in some special-category states), or if you sell across states or through e-commerce marketplaces. Check with your CA for your case.

Can I use this for a GST invoice?

Yes. Use our free GST invoice generator to create a printable invoice with HSN codes, tax split and amount in words.

Is GST calculated on MRP?

MRP in India already includes GST. To find the GST inside it, use "Remove GST": for an MRP of ₹1,180 at 18%, the GST is ₹180.

What is the GST rate on services?

Most services, including IT, consulting, marketing and professional services, are taxed at 18%. Some, like restaurants and certain transport services, are at 5%.

How often do I file GST returns?

Most regular taxpayers file GSTR-1 (sales) and GSTR-3B (summary and payment) monthly, or quarterly under the QRMP scheme if turnover is up to ₹5 crore.
