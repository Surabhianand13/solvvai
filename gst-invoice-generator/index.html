What a GST invoice must include

Under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, a tax invoice needs:

  • Your name, address and GSTIN
  • A unique invoice number (up to 16 characters, one series per financial year) and the date
  • Customer name and address, and their GSTIN if they are registered
  • Place of supply (state name and code) for inter-state sales
  • HSN code for goods or SAC code for services
  • Description, quantity, unit and value of each item, and any discount
  • Taxable value, GST rate, and tax amount split into CGST + SGST or IGST
  • Signature or digital signature of the supplier

This generator fills in the tax split for you. If your state and the place of supply match, it charges CGST + SGST. If they differ, it charges IGST. Type a GSTIN and the state is picked up from its first two digits.

Tax invoice or bill of supply?

Registered businesses charging GST issue a tax invoice. Businesses under the composition scheme, or selling only exempt goods, issue a bill of supply without GST. Untick "Charge GST" above to make one.

How to create a GST invoice in 2 minutes

  1. Add your business details: name, address, GSTIN and state. Upload your logo if you have one.
  2. Add the customer and their GSTIN if they are registered. The place of supply sets IGST or CGST + SGST automatically.
  3. Add items with HSN/SAC code, quantity, rate, discount and GST rate.
  4. Add payment details and terms, such as bank account, UPI ID and due date.
  5. Click Download PDF and send it to your customer on WhatsApp or email.

Invoice numbering rules

  • Up to 16 characters, using letters, numbers, hyphen (-) and slash (/).
  • Unique for each financial year, in one or more series, e.g. INV/26-27/001.
  • No gaps or duplicates. Cancelled invoices should be recorded, not reused.

When to issue the invoice

SupplyTime limit

Copies of a GST invoice

For goods: original for the buyer, duplicate for the transporter, and triplicate for your records. For services: original for the buyer and duplicate for you. A PDF from this generator works for all of these.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator free?

Yes. Your first invoice PDF is free. Share your contact details once to download it. Need invoices every day? We can set up automated billing for your business.

Where is my data stored?

Nowhere. The invoice is built in your browser and disappears once you download it. Nothing you type into the invoice is saved or sent to our servers.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Click Download PDF and choose Save as PDF as the printer. On Android or iPhone, use Print → Save as PDF, or share it straight to WhatsApp.

Do I need an e-invoice (IRN)?

E-invoicing on the government IRP portal applies to businesses with aggregate turnover above ₹5 crore in any year since 2017-18. Smaller businesses can use a regular tax invoice like this one.

Can I make a quotation instead?

Yes. Use our free quotation maker. It has the same layout with validity dates and quotation terms.

Is HSN code mandatory on invoices?

Yes, for most businesses. Turnover up to ₹5 crore must show 4-digit HSN/SAC on B2B invoices; above ₹5 crore, 6 digits on all invoices.

Can I issue an invoice without GST?

Yes, if you are not registered or under the composition scheme. Untick "Charge GST" to create a plain invoice or bill of supply.

Do I need a signature on a PDF invoice?

A GST invoice needs the supplier’s signature or digital signature. E-invoices with an IRN do not need a separate signature.
