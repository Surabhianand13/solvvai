EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) P = loan amount · r = yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100 · n = months

Example: ₹30 lakh for 20 years at 8.5% gives an EMI of ₹26,035. Over 240 months you repay ₹62.48 lakh, of which ₹32.48 lakh is interest.

EMI calculation example, step by step

Say you borrow ₹10 lakh at 10% for 5 years:

P = 10,00,000 r = 10 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.008333 per month n = 5 × 12 = 60 months EMI = 10,00,000 × 0.008333 × (1.008333)⁶⁰ ÷ ((1.008333)⁶⁰ − 1) = ₹21,247

Over 60 months you pay ₹12.75 lakh in total, so the loan costs you ₹2.75 lakh in interest.

5 ways to pay less on your loan

  1. Improve your credit score before you apply. A CIBIL score above 750 gets the lowest rates.
  2. Make a bigger down payment so you borrow less.
  3. Choose the shortest tenure you can afford. Use the table above to see the trade-off.
  4. Prepay when you can. On a ₹30 lakh, 20-year loan at 8.5%, prepaying ₹1 lakh at the end of every year closes the loan in under 12 years and saves about ₹14.8 lakh in interest.
  5. Negotiate or transfer. If rates fall, ask your bank to reprice, or move to a cheaper lender.

Frequently asked questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1). This calculator does it for you and also shows how each year’s payments split between principal and interest.

Why is most of my early EMI going to interest?

Interest is charged on the outstanding balance, which is highest at the start. As you repay principal, the interest part of each EMI shrinks.

Should I prepay my loan?

Prepaying early in the tenure saves the most interest. Home loans on floating rates have no prepayment penalty for individuals.

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Yes, but you pay much more interest overall. Choose the shortest tenure whose EMI you can comfortably afford.

What is the EMI for a ₹10 lakh loan for 5 years?

At 10% a year, the EMI is ₹21,247. At 12% it is about ₹22,244. Use the calculator above for your exact rate.

What is the EMI for a ₹20 lakh home loan?

At 8.5% for 20 years, the EMI on ₹20 lakh is about ₹17,356.

Is EMI calculated on reducing balance?

Yes. Banks in India calculate EMI on the reducing balance, so interest is charged only on the amount still outstanding. This calculator uses the same method.
