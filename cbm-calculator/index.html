How to calculate CBM

CBM per carton = Length × Width × Height (in metres) From cm: L × W × H ÷ 1,000,000 From inch: L × W × H ÷ 61,024 Total CBM = CBM per carton × number of cartons CFT = CBM × 35.315

Example: 100 cartons of 50 × 40 × 30 cm = 0.06 CBM each = 6 CBM in total (211.9 CFT). That fits comfortably in LCL or a 20ft container.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CBM and CFT?

Both measure volume. CBM is cubic metres and CFT is cubic feet. 1 CBM = 35.315 CFT. Indian transporters often quote in CFT; international freight uses CBM.

What is W/M or revenue ton in LCL?

Sea LCL is billed on whichever is higher: volume in CBM or weight in tonnes. 3 CBM weighing 1,800 kg is billed as 3 revenue tons; 3 CBM weighing 4,000 kg is billed as 4.

How is air freight chargeable weight calculated?

Air cargo uses volumetric weight = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6,000, and couriers usually ÷ 5,000. You pay on the higher of actual or volumetric weight. Try our volumetric weight calculator.
