How gold jewellery price is calculated

Rate per gram (purity) = 24K rate per gram × purity ÷ 999 Gold value = Rate per gram × weight Wastage = Gold value × wastage % Making charges = % of gold value, or ₹ per gram, or flat GST = 3% × (gold + wastage + making + stones + hallmark) Final price = Subtotal + GST − old gold value

Example: at a 24K rate of ₹1,20,000 per 10g, 22K gold costs about ₹11,003 per gram. A 10g chain with 12% making charges comes to ₹1,10,030 + ₹13,204 making = ₹1,23,234, plus 3% GST = ₹1,26,931.

Frequently asked questions

What are typical making charges?

Usually 6–25% of the gold value, depending on design. Machine-made chains sit at the low end; intricate handmade and temple jewellery at the high end. Some jewellers charge a fixed ₹ per gram instead.

Is hallmarking mandatory?

Yes. BIS hallmarking with a 6-digit HUID is mandatory for gold jewellery sold in India (with small exemptions such as items under 2 grams). The hallmarking charge is about ₹45 per piece.

Is GST charged on old gold exchange?

No. GST applies to the new jewellery you buy. The old gold value is deducted from the final bill.

Where do I find today's gold rate?

Check the IBJA rate or your local jeweller's board. Rates change daily and vary slightly by city.
