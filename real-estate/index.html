Most residential developers are running Google and Meta campaigns, generating hundreds of leads a month — but only a handful ever show up for a site visit. The ad spend isn't the problem. The funnel is — and it's leaking at five specific points.

Ads Attracting the Wrong People

No Lead Qualification Layer
