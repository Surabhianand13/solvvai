How an SWP works

Each month a fixed amount is redeemed from your fund. The remaining balance stays invested and keeps earning returns. If returns exceed withdrawals, your corpus can last for life.

Example: ₹50 lakh with ₹30,000 a month withdrawn (7.2% a year) at 9% returns still has about ₹98.6 lakh left after 20 years, because returns outpace the withdrawals.

Frequently asked questions

SWP or FD interest?

SWP from a debt or hybrid fund is often more tax-efficient, since only gains are taxed, not the whole withdrawal.

Which funds suit an SWP?

Balanced advantage, hybrid or debt funds are common choices because they are less volatile than pure equity.

What if markets fall?

Withdrawing during a fall sells more units, so keep 1–2 years of expenses in a safer fund to draw from.
