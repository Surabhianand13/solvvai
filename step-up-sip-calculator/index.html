Why step-up SIPs work

Your income rises over time, so your SIP should too. A yearly increase in line with your salary hike puts more money to work in the later years, when compounding is strongest.

Example: ₹10,000 a month at 12% for 20 years gives about ₹99.9 lakh as a flat SIP. With a 10% yearly step-up it grows to about ₹1.99 crore.

Frequently asked questions

What step-up percentage should I choose?

Match it to your expected salary growth. 5–10% a year is common.

Is a step-up SIP better than a lumpsum top-up?

A step-up is automatic and disciplined. Occasional lumpsum top-ups also help when you get a bonus.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns vary with the market; the calculator assumes a steady rate.
